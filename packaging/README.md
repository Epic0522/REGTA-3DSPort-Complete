# HOME Menu artwork and CIA packaging

The public packaging inputs are in [prebuilt](prebuilt/README.md):
three finished animated CGFX banners, their encoded audio and their icons.
They are ready to use with the compiled game ELFs.

Follow [production_cia/README.md](production_cia/README.md) to build the
packages. Blender and the original game models are not needed.

The `tools` folder keeps the artwork utilities for reference. The old
`gta3`, `vc`, `lcs`, `.tools` and test-package directories are local
working files, ignored by Git. They are not dependencies of the public
packaging script. Generated CIA, 3DSX, BNR and SMDH files are also ignored.
